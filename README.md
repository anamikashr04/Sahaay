# Sahaay Care System

Interactive mobile app prototype of the Sahaay Care System, a continuous care support system for St. Jude India ChildCare Centres.

**Team Sahaay** · ServDes India Student Service Design Competition 2026

- App: `index.html`
- Website query form (no app download needed): `index.html#query`
- Staff demo PIN: **1234**

## Roles
- **Parent:** simple picture-based requests in English and Hindi, "Ask staff to call me", and a detailed view
- **Contributor:** urgent needs, what centres are running low on (live from inventory), giving history
- **Volunteer:** onboarding, verification, yes/no polls, my history, workshop proposals
- **Staff (PIN-protected):** one dashboard per centre with Approvals, Queries inbox, Inventory, Repairs & complaints, Needs for donors, Night care, Yes/No polls and Records
- **Night Care:** parent support from 6 PM to 10 AM, when no staff are on site

## Changes from St. Jude staff feedback (Oct 2026)
| Feedback | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| Staff can't see past records in their current app | **Records:** searchable history of every donation, request, stock entry, query, alert and poll |
| Not everyone will download the app | **Website query page** (`#query`) that feeds the same Staff inbox |
| Everything should be in one window | **Staff dashboard** with all sections and counts on one screen |
| Inward/outward register of goods per centre | **Inventory:** inward/outward entries per centre, with available / not available |
| Red mark for items to procure again | **Red dot** on items to procure; clears when procured or restocked; "Ask donors" posts it as a need |
| Yes/no poll | **Yes / No polls** for staff and volunteers |
| Stock and complaints | **Repairs & complaints** log with Repair, Complaint and Stock types |
| Parents have low digital literacy | **Simple mode** for parents (big picture buttons, Hindi labels); staff can raise requests for a parent |
| Staff use their own phones; data should be secure | **Staff PIN sign-in** (demo) |
| Too many calls to staff after work | Queries and call-back requests go to the inbox; office hours 10 AM – 6 PM |
